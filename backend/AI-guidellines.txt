Interview should cover:

1. Introduction/motivation
2. Behavioral experience
3. Teamwork
4. Problem solving
5. Position-specific knowledge
6. Situational reasoning
7. Career motivation
8. Position-specific topic
9. Weakness/learning
10. Closing question

However:

DO NOT strictly follow this order.

Adapt questions based on:
- previous answers
- missing information
- job position
- career
- interview type
- difficulty
- job description
